## Step 1: Survey and plan (1:05-2:00)
> Step one is the survey, and it happens before any demolition. We check the walls for damp. We check where the pipes run, test the drain, and look at the electrics. In Cyprus, many older bathrooms have old galvanised or poorly joined pipework. Sometimes there is a shower tray that has been leaking quietly for years. Then we agree the layout with you. Moving a toilet or a shower costs more than keeping it in place, so it is worth deciding early. If anything structural is involved, or the building has shared walls or shared drains, speak to the building owner or management committee first, and check whether any permit or approval is needed. We are always happy to explain, in plain language, what is likely to apply to your building.

## Step 4: Waterproofing, the step nobody sees (3:45-4:35)
> Here is the step we promised. Waterproofing. After the walls and floor are levelled, we apply a waterproof membrane to the shower area and the floor, and seal the corners and joints with tape. Then we let it cure fully, following the manufacturer's instructions. You never see this layer once the tiles are on, and that is exactly why people skip it. When it fails, the water goes into the wall, or through to the neighbour below. The tiles still look fine. The damage does not. This is the most expensive shortcut in any bathroom. Waterproofing is part of our everyday work, in bathrooms, terraces and roofs, so we never treat it as an optional extra.

## Step 5: Tiling (4:35-5:15)
> Step five is tiling. Good tiling is mostly preparation. Flat surfaces, the correct adhesive for the tile type, even spacing, and a plan for where the cuts fall, so you do not end up with thin slivers by the door. Large format tiles look modern, but they need a very flat surface. Then comes the grout. In the corners, and wherever two surfaces meet, we use a flexible sealant, not grout. Rigid grout in a corner will crack. It is a small detail, and it matters a great deal. Details like this are what quality workmanship really means.

**On-screen text:** "5. Tiling" / "Flexible sealant in corners".
