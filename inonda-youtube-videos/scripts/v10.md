## 1:25-2:48 Boilers and water heating

> Let us start with boilers and water heating. Many homes in Cyprus use a solar water heater with an electric element as backup, and others use gas or electric boilers. So the details vary, but the logic is the same. First, age. If the unit is old and has needed repeated repairs, each repair is buying you less time. Second, the fault. A failed thermostat or a simple part is usually a repair. A leaking tank or heavy internal corrosion usually means replacement, because the tank itself cannot be fixed economically. Third, cost. A rule of thumb many tradespeople use is that if the repair is getting close to a large share of the price of a new unit, replacement deserves serious thought. Fourth, safety. Never ignore gas smells, scorch marks or electrical problems. Switch it off and call a qualified person. In coastal areas, salt air can age metal parts faster, so ask the technician what they see on yours. And if your roof is part of the picture, for example where a solar water heater sits, we can also check the roof and waterproofing around it.

## 3:05-4:22 Windows

> Windows. Here the questions are a little different. What is actually wrong? If the frame is solid and the problem is a worn seal, a stiff handle, a failed hinge or a misaligned sash, repair is often sensible. Condensation between two panes of double glazing usually means the seal has failed, and that unit typically needs replacing, though often not the whole frame. If the frame itself is warped, rotting, badly corroded or cracked, repairs tend to be short-lived. Then think about comfort. If your home is hot in summer, cold and damp in winter, and noisy, an old single-glazed window may be costing you in energy and comfort every month. A leaking window can also let water into the wall around it, which is where our damp treatment and painting work often begins. So ask for a quote for both options, repairing the existing windows and replacing them, so you can compare honestly. Replacement price will depend on size, material and glass type, so ask for it itemised, and ask about lead times and installation too.

## 4:22-5:32 Flooring

> Now flooring. The big question here is whether the problem is a surface issue or a base issue. One or two cracked or loose tiles can often be replaced, as long as you can match them, and that is a tricky point with older tiles. If many tiles are cracking, sounding hollow, or lifting, that can point to a problem underneath, such as movement, damp, or poor adhesion. Replacing tiles on top just repeats the failure. Scratched or worn wooden or laminate floors may be sanded, refinished or patched, depending on the type, while swollen boards after water damage usually need replacing along with finding the leak. Our advice is simple. Fix the cause first, then decide on the floor. If you are unsure, ask your contractor to explain why they think it has failed, in plain language. At Inonda, that is exactly what we aim to do, because you should always understand what you are paying for.
