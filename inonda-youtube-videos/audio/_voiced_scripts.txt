Most people hire a builder without knowing what happens behind the scenes. How is the price worked out? What if something goes wrong? Today, we show you how Inonda Ltd plans, quotes and runs a job in Cyprus.

We're Inonda Ltd. We handle property repairs, renovations and maintenance across Cyprus, from roofing and waterproofing to bathrooms, kitchens, damp treatment and painting. Inonda is a new, owner-run company, and the technician in charge of every job has up to 22 years of hands-on repair experience. This video isn't a sales pitch. It's a walk through our process, so you can judge whether it's how you'd want your home handled. We'll also point out what you can do to make any job go better, because a good job is a team effort.

Step one is the enquiry. It can come by phone, WhatsApp or through the channel. We ask for a short description, photos if possible, and the location. We use that to decide what kind of visit is needed. Then we visit, because we don't believe in quoting a repair we haven't seen. We look at the problem, the surrounding area, access, and anything that might affect the work, like the age of the property, the materials, and whether the space is occupied. Our local knowledge of Cyprus homes and climate helps here. We take measurements and photos for our notes, and we ask what you actually want. Sometimes the best answer is a small repair, sometimes a bigger fix. We explain both, honestly, and tell you which one we'd choose, and why.

Step two is the quote. After the visit, we write it up. We break the work into stages and list the materials we expect to use, the labour, and the time we think it will take. We list what's included, and what isn't. Where there's a real unknown, like something hidden behind a wall, we say so up front and explain how we'd handle it. We won't promise what we can't know. A quote is only useful if you understand it, so we're happy to go through it line by line. If you receive other quotes, compare the scope, not just the total. A cheaper quote that leaves out half the work isn't cheaper.

Step three is planning, the part clients rarely see. Once a quote is accepted, we agree a start date and a realistic timeline. We order materials early where we can, because waiting for a delivery is a common cause of delay. We plan the order of work so one stage doesn't undo another. We think about your daily life: when you need the kitchen, the bathroom, or the water on. And we plan how to protect your home, with floor covers, dust control and a tidy working area.

Step four is the work itself. Each day, we arrive when we said we would, protect the area, and work through the stages in order. We keep the site safe and tidy, and we clean up at the end of every day. Quality workmanship is mostly this: each stage done properly, in the right order. We keep you informed with a short message and a photo, so nobody has to chase us. If something unexpected comes up, we stop, show you what we found, explain the options, and agree what to do before we continue. We never quietly change the plan. If the job needs more time or more work, you hear it from us first, with the reason.

Step five is the handover. We walk through the finished work with you, check it against the quote, and test what can be tested. We explain how to look after the new work, for example how long paint or sealant needs to cure. If something isn't right, we put it right. We'd much rather fix a small problem now than have you live with it.
