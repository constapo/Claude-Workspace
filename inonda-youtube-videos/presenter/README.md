# Presenter reference (owner's AI likeness)

The owner chose an **AI version of themselves** as presenter (2026-10-12) and consented to
their face being used. Photos and video of the owner are personal data. They are **not
committed to git** (see `.gitignore` here). Keep the originals in Google Drive or Dropbox
and attach them to the chat when a session needs them.

## What to provide
- 10–20 photos: face clearly visible, front, ¾ left, ¾ right, profile; daylight or soft
  light; no sunglasses or hats; neutral expression plus a few smiling; plain background.
- 1 video, 30–60 s: talking to camera at normal pace, phone at eye level, quiet room.
- The work clothes you want to appear in (one consistent outfit for all videos).

## Process (credit rule applies)
1. Check the photos (sharpness, lighting, angles) – free.
2. Price one test talking clip (≈5–8 s, a V01 line) on the chosen service – show the price.
3. With approval: make that one clip, QA it (looks exactly like the owner, lips in sync,
   no warping), show it, wait.
4. With approval: the presenter shots in videos 01–16 and 18, in batches.
Every clip of the AI owner carries a small "AI-generated" label.
