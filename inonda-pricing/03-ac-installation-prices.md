# Air-conditioning installation: Cyprus market prices (2026-11-18)

Scope: supply and install one inverter split set (indoor unit + outdoor compressor), wall-mounted,
standard installation with up to 3 m of pipe between the units. Inonda supplies the unit (materials
included). Installation is done by a licensed subcontractor (AC work in the EU needs an F-gas certified
technician), so the owner adds **20%** on top of the market price. That 20% is internal and is never shown
to clients; the client sees one final price including 19% VAT.

## How the figures were built
- **Unit prices** (retail, VAT included), checked 2026-11-18:
  - kyriakoselectric.com: Sensair 9k €349 / 12k €369 / 18k €599 / 24k €779; Midea Solunar €445 /
    €469 / €759 / €959; Midea Solstice €499–535 / €529–590 / €800–859 / €1,050–1,080.
  - andreascharalambouscy.com: Omnys €319 / €339 / €539 / €619; Midea Solunar €399 / €419 / €679 /
    €879; Midea Solstice €519 / €539 / €799 / €959.
  - "Typical" = mid-range Midea Solunar class (A++, R32, Wi-Fi). Low = budget brand. High = premium
    Midea Solstice.
- **Installation labour**:
  - cmcelectric.com: 9k–12k €130, 18k €140, extra pipe beyond 3 m €20 per metre, brick wall with no
    pipe provision +€20, disconnection €20–25.
  - douleutaras.com.cy: up to 14,000 BTU €120, up to 24,000 BTU €140, removal from €30.
  - 3stepscooling.com: supply and installation "starting at €550".

## Estimates and limits (not presented as fact)
- Neither installer page says whether its labour price includes VAT. I treated it as VAT-included
  consumer pricing. If it is excluding VAT, the client prices go up by about €25–30 per set.
- The prices are for a standard installation. Ducted, cassette and multi-split systems, long pipe runs,
  roof or crane access, and a new dedicated electrical line are not covered. Those stay
  "price on request".
- Retail prices change with seasonal promotions (several of the listings are sale prices), so this table
  should be checked again before the summer season.
- With the 20% added, Inonda's price sits at or slightly above the top of the market for 18k and 24k
  units. The owner may want to round these down or use the budget brand for those sizes.
